# Open questions

While a question is open, nobody implements or invents anything for it. Specs that depend on it are marked **BLOCKED** (`backlog-to-spec`), and `domain-glossary` §5 lists what stays out of the code.

When the team decides, write the answer here with the date, update `domain-glossary` §5 and the affected specs **in the same PR**. A partial decision records what was decided; the rest stays BLOCKED.

All seven questions were decided by the team on 2026-09-28 (confirmed by Lorenzo). No question is open; the table is kept as a decision log. A new question gets the next id (Q8) and status "Open".

| Id | Question | Stories | Status | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Is the collaborator a fifth actor, or are its stories removed? | KAN-78 to KAN-86 (also KAN-61, 66, 67, 68, 103, 105, 136, 137, 138, 142, 152, the collaborator limit of KAN-181) | Decided | 2026-09-28: the collaborator is a fifth actor, an employee of the business who serves customers (for example a barber in a barbershop). Role `collaborator` with the `businessId` claim (and `collaboratorId`). The collaborator uses the business portal, limited to what the subscriber enables for them (KAN-86, `COLLABORATOR_PERMISSION`). `Collaborator` lives at `businesses/{businessId}/collaborators/{collaboratorId}` with a linked `userId`; statuses `invited → active ↔ inactive`. `Booking.collaboratorId` records who serves each booking. Each collaborator serves one booking at a time. Plans limit the number of collaborators (`limits.maxCollaborators`, KAN-181); reaching the limit offers an upgrade (KAN-85). |
| Q2 | Does a `pending` status exist for a business? | KAN-175, KAN-176 | Decided | 2026-09-28: yes. After the plan checkout (Q7) and the subscriber sign-up, the new business is `pending` ("paid, awaiting approval") and the subscriber sees an "under review" notice. The super admin approves it (`pending → active`, email to the owner, full access) or rejects it with a mandatory reason (`pending → rejected`, terminal, email to the owner with the reason). KAN-176 changes: the business activates on approval, not on payment. KAN-175's `pending` filter means "awaiting approval". Decided together with Q3. |
| Q3 | Which "approvals" does the audit log record? | KAN-194, KAN-195 | Decided | 2026-09-28: the approvals are the approval and rejection of new businesses (Q2). `AuditLogEntry.actionType` values, from KAN-194's list: business approved, business rejected, business suspended, business reactivated, plan created, plan updated, plan activated, plan deactivated, plan price changed, platform settings updated (`AUDIT_LOG_ACTION_TYPE`). |
| Q4 | How is a business page reached: slug in the URL, subdomain or a search? | KAN-111 epic, customer portal routes | Decided | 2026-09-28: by link with a slug in the path, `sitio.com/<business-slug>`. `Business.slug` is unique, lowercase and URL-safe; the customer portal lives under `/:businessSlug/...`. Static top-level routes (landing, auth, business, admin) win over the slug segment, and their segments are reserved slugs (`domain-glossary` §3). The slug is chosen at subscriber sign-up (KAN-25, Q7), checked against reserved slugs and uniqueness, and cannot be changed afterwards. |
| Q5 | What does the simulated gateway do for automatic renewals? | KAN-48 (duplicate: KAN-50) | Decided | 2026-09-28: the simulated gateway only fakes a successful or a failed payment. Automatic renewal retries (KAN-48, duplicate KAN-50) are deferred until the project is further along (out of MVP). `past_due` stays in the subscription machine, but nothing in the MVP drives retries. |
| Q6 | Can a visitor book without an account? | KAN-116 | Decided | 2026-09-28: no, a customer account is mandatory. The customer gives full name and phone number, plus the email and password of the account. A visitor who starts a booking signs in or signs up and returns to the booking flow at the same service. `Booking` has no guest fields. |
| Q7 | Does the MVP include plan checkout with the gateway, or start with a business created by hand? | KAN-20 epic (KAN-21 to KAN-24), KAN-2, KAN-7, KAN-25, KAN-27, KAN-33, KAN-176 | Decided | 2026-09-28: the MVP includes plan checkout from the landing with the simulated gateway (which only fakes a success or a failure, Q5). The visitor chooses a plan, accepts the terms and pays (KAN-22, KAN-23); the confirmation email (KAN-24) links to the subscriber sign-up (KAN-25), where the business name and `slug` are set; the new business is `pending` until the super admin approves it (Q2). |
